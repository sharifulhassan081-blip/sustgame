# SUST – Sylhet BRTC Double-Decker Bus Simulator (ভাড়া ৫ টাকা)

An interactive 3D **BRTC Double-Decker Bus Simulator** set across recognizable streets and landmarks of **Sylhet, Bangladesh** — modeled after real Sylhet road topology from **SUST (Shahjalal University of Science & Technology)** to **Madina Market**, **Ambarkhana**, **Zindabazar**, **Bondor Bazar**, and **Tilagor**.

## Key Features

- **Iconic BRTC Red & Green Double-Decker Bus**:
  - Two-story passenger cabins (Lower Deck + Upper Deck) that visibly fill with 3D students as they board.
  - Dynamic digital LED destination boards, working pneumatic left-side folding door, right-hand-drive driver cockpit with animated steering wheel & rain wipers, headlights, brake lights, and dual-tone BRTC hydraulic air horn.
- **5 Tk Student Fare System (`ভাড়া ৫ টাকা`)**:
  - Pull into glowing green bus stop bays along your route, press **`O`** (or tap **OPEN DOOR**), watch SUST & Sylhet college students walk aboard, and collect **৳5 Tk** per student!
- **Living Sylhet Street Traffic**:
  - Colorful **Sylheti Cycle Rickshaws** (with folk-art hoods and animated pedaling rickshaw pullers) and **Emerald-Green Sylheti CNG Auto-Rickshaws** (with protective side grills) driving in authentic Bangladesh left-hand traffic.
  - Honk your **BRTC Horn (`H`)** to alert rickshaws and CNGs ahead so they pull closer to the curb!
- **4 Multi-Stop Sylhet Routes**:
  1. `BRTC-01`: **SUST ⇄ Madina Market ⇄ Ambarkhana ⇄ Zindabazar ⇄ Bondor Bazar**
  2. `BRTC-02`: **SUST ⇄ Madina Market ⇄ Ambarkhana ⇄ Shahi Eidgah ⇄ Tilagor**
  3. `BRTC-03`: **Tilagor ⇄ Shibganj ⇄ Zindabazar ⇄ Ambarkhana ⇄ SUST**
  4. `BRTC-04`: **Grand All-Sylhet Tour**
- **Recognizable Sylhet Landmarks**:
  - **SUST Gol Chattar (Chetona '71)**, **1-Kilometer Road & Canals**, and **SUST Main Gate**
  - **BGB Sylhet Sector Gate (Akhalia)**
  - **Mount Adora Hospital, Akhalia** (with 24/7 Emergency bay & ambulance)
  - **Madina Market Point** (Walton Showroom, bazaar stalls & Ragib-Rabeya Medical Rd sign)
  - **Shwapno Super Shop** outlets at **Sunrise Tower (Pathantula)**, **East Zindabazar**, and **Shibganj**
  - **Tarapur & Lakkatura Tea Garden Hills**
  - **Dorshon Dewri (Hazrat Shahjalal R. Mazar Gate)** & **Subidbazar**
  - **Ambarkhana Point & Arcadia Mall**
  - **Sylhet Central Shaheed Minar (Chouhatta)**
  - **Al-Hamra Shopping City**, **Blue Water Shopping City** & **Panshi Restaurant (Zindabazar)**
  - **Sylhet City Corporation Nagar Bhaban**, **Hasan Market**, **Ali Amjad's Clock Tower (1872)** & **Keane Bridge over the Surma River (Bondor Bazar)**
  - **Historic Shahi Eidgah** & **Murari Chand (MC) College, Tilagor (1892)**

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch / Mouse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accelerate / Brake & Reverse | `W` / `S` or `↑` / `↓` | `GAS` / `BRAKE` Pedals |
| Steer Left / Right | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` | `◀` / `▶` Buttons |
| Open / Close Boarding Door (`৳5 Tk`) | `O` or `E` | `OPEN DOOR` Button |
| BRTC Hydraulic Air Horn | `H` | `HORN` Button |
| Switch Camera (Chase / Driver Cabin / 2nd Deck / Aerial) | `C` | Camera Button |
| Toggle Sylhet Weather (Day / Sunset / Monsoon Rain) | — | Weather Button |
| Full Sylhet GPS Route & Landmark Map | `M` | Minimap / `Routes & Map` |
| Reset Bus to Nearest Road | `R` | `Reset` Button |
